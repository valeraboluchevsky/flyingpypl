The way to fly

Want to fly better? You’re in the right place. Everyone here is a little obsessed with flying, so you’ll fit right in.

  • The windThe wind is the only reason we fly. So you have to understand it and make friends with it, not fight it.
  • The mixAnything that helps you fly better is useful. Don’t let trends get into your head.
  • The languageFlying is like speaking a foreign language: practice has to be regular. Even a little is fine, as long as you keep flying. That is how the basics really stick.
